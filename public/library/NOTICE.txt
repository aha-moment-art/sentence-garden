素材来源与许可 / Source notices

This catalog reuses the owner's existing public projects, pinned to the revisions in index.json.
Audio is streamed from the original files at those immutable GitHub revisions. No new speech is generated.
Counts preserve original dictionary/date groupings and include duplicates across groups.

British Ear: https://github.com/aha-moment-art/british-ear
BritSpeak: https://github.com/aha-moment-art/BritSpeak
Original speech clips, supplied transcripts and translations. Rights in underlying recordings remain with their respective owners; this integration does not assign them a new license.

WordLeap: https://github.com/aha-moment-art/WordLeap
Dictionary definitions: ECDICT, MIT license, reproduced in licenses/ECDICT-LICENSE.txt.
PTE dictionary: Qwerty Learner, GPL-3.0, reproduced in licenses/QWERTY-LEARNER-GPL-3.0.txt.
Corresponding source dictionary data is preserved in WordLeap/public/dicts at the revision listed in index.json; transformation source is scripts/import-projects.mjs in Sentence Garden.
Tatoeba sentences: CC BY 2.0 FR, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Each Tatoeba example retains its sentence ID, contributor name when supplied, and link to the original sentence. Sentence text is unchanged; metadata is reformatted for practice.
Original generated example/word recordings are reused, without regeneration.

Level Up Cards: https://github.com/aha-moment-art/level-up-cards
Original vocabulary cards retain examples and translations. UK/US pronunciation belongs to WORD entries, not sentence examples.
Daily complete listening recordings retain the supplied BBC source links. These are separate from sentence/word recordings. Rights remain with the original owners.

Sentence Garden code and original example content do not change the licenses of imported source materials.
